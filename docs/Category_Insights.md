# Phase 2: Category & Margin Leakage Audit Report

## Key Findings & Recommendations

### 1. SKU & Category Rankings
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** High-frequency essentials like Dairy and Bakery drive steady volume across both In-Store and Online channels.
* **Channel Split:** Online orders exhibit higher average net transaction values, led by premium SKU categories such as Beverages.

### 2. Margin Leakage & Discount Depth Analysis
* **Identified Leakage:** Discounts exceeding 15% on high-cost SKUs cause severe margin compression without delivering a proportional increase in basket size.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Implement strict discount caps on low-margin SKUs and shift focus toward multi-item bundling rather than flat price reductions.
