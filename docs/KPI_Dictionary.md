# RetailPulse KPI Dictionary & Formula Guide

## 1. Financial Baseline Metrics
* **Gross Sales:** `Units Sold * Unit Price`
* **Net Sales:** `Gross Sales - Discount Amount`
* **Gross Margin ($):** `Net Sales - COGS`
* **Gross Margin (%):** `(Gross Margin / Net Sales) * 100`

## 2. Merchandising Metrics
* **Average Basket Value (ABV):** `Total Net Sales / Total Unique Transactions`
* **Basket Size (Units per Basket):** `Total Units Sold / Total Unique Transactions`
* **Discount Depth (%):** `(Total Discount Amount / Total Gross Sales) * 100`
